WHO WE ARE

Your Trusted Technical Infrastructure Partner

Avent Technical Group LTD is a 100% Canadian-owned and operated company proudly based in London, Ontario. For over 20 years, we have been serving London and Southwestern Ontario as a trusted contractor specializing in network infrastructure and integrated security systems.

As a local business, we take great pride in supporting our community while delivering the highest standards of workmanship and reliability. Our core services include structured cabling systems (copper and fiber optic networks), backbone infrastructure, and the supply, installation, and maintenance of access control and CCTV security systems.

Avent has extensive experience managing large, complex projects that require coordination, compliance, security clearance standards, and strict timelines. We work closely with consultants, general contractors, project managers, and ownership groups during the design and build phases, ensuring systems are installed efficiently, correctly, and future ready.

Our long-standing relationships with leading manufacturers in the industry allow us to provide reliable, scalable solutions backed by strong warranties and ongoing support. In addition to new installations, we provide service, upgrades, and maintenance for existing infrastructure, helping clients protect and extend the life of their systems.
